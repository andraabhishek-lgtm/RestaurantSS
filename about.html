Behind the Scene

Where Every Meal
Tells a Story

ÉCLAT was born from a single obsession: that a meal should feel like a moment stolen from ordinary life. Founded in 2018 by Chef Laurent Dubois in the heart of New York, we set out to create a space where French technique meets the precision of Japanese craft — where every ingredient earns its place on your plate, and every detail in the room earns its place in your memory.

"Cooking is not a performance. It is a quiet conversation between the kitchen and the soul of the guest."
— Chef Laurent Dubois

Six years on, two Michelin Stars later, our philosophy has never wavered. We believe the finest dining is not spectacle — it is intimacy, built course by course, in the language of flavour, texture, and care.

What We Believe

Our Philosophy

Provenance First

Every ingredient is sourced with intention — from micro-season farms in Burgundy to coastal fisheries in Hokkaido. We know the name of the farmer behind every dish.

Craft Without Compromise

Our kitchen operates at the intersection of classical French rigour and Japanese minimalism. Technique serves the ingredient — never the other way around.

Hospitality as Art

We believe that how you are made to feel is as important as what you taste. Every guest is welcomed as if the restaurant were our home and they our dearest friend.

Our Journey

Milestones

2018

ÉCLAT opens its doors on Lumière Blvd, New York — a 48-seat room with an 8-course tasting menu.

2020

Awarded our first Michelin Star — recognised for "singular precision and quiet audacity."

2022

The Bar at ÉCLAT opens alongside our guest rooms — turning dinner into a full overnight experience.

2024

Second Michelin Star awarded. Named "Best Fine Dining Experience" by the New York Culinary Guild.
