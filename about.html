The Beginning

A Vision Born from Passion

ÉCLAT was born in 2018 from Chef Laurent Dubois's lifelong dream to create a space where French haute cuisine techniques met the bold, layered flavors of Asia. Having trained in Paris, Tokyo, and Hong Kong, Laurent returned to New York with an unprecedented vision.

The name ÉCLAT — French for "brilliance" and "splendor" — captures the essence of what we strive to create with every dish: a moment of pure, blinding excellence.

Within our first year, we earned a Michelin Star. By 2023, we received our second, cementing ÉCLAT as one of New York's most celebrated dining destinations.

Our Philosophy

Guided by Core Values

Farm to Table

Every ingredient is sourced from trusted local farms and sustainable fisheries. We believe great food starts with great produce.

Artisan Craft

Our kitchen is a studio. Every technique, every plating decision, every flavor combination is the result of meticulous craftsmanship.

Sustainability

Zero food waste is our goal. We compost, repurpose, and minimize our environmental footprint at every step of the culinary process.

Hospitality

We believe dining is an act of love. From the moment you enter to the final goodbye, you are our most honored guest.

Our Journey

A Legacy of Excellence

2018

Grand Opening

ÉCLAT opens its doors on Lumière Boulevard with a 12-dish tasting menu that immediately captures the attention of New York's culinary world.

2019

First Michelin Star

Just one year after opening, ÉCLAT is awarded its first Michelin Star — the fastest in NYC history at the time.

2021

Private Dining Suite Launch

We open our exclusive 12-seat private dining suite — Le Salon Privé — for corporate and intimate celebrations.

2023

Second Michelin Star

ÉCLAT achieves two Michelin Stars, joining the elite tier of New York's finest restaurants and becoming a global culinary destination.

2024

Expansion & Innovation

Launch of our culinary studio for masterclasses, and an expanded beverage program curated by our award-winning sommelier.
