Our story

ÉCLAT was born from a single obsession: that a meal should feel like a moment stolen from ordinary life. Founded in 2018 by Chef Laurent Dubois in the heart of New York, we set out to create a place where French technique meets the precision of Japanese craft — where every ingredient has a reason to be on your plate, and every detail in the room has a reason to be in your memory.

Six years on, two Michelin Stars later, our philosophy hasn't changed. We believe the finest dining is not a performance — it is a quiet conversation between the kitchen and the guest, conducted in the language of flavour, texture, and care.
