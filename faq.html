Reservations

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 2–4 weeks in advance, especially for weekends and holidays. Tasting menu experiences and special events may require 4–6 weeks lead time. Same-week bookings are sometimes available — call us directly for last-minute availability.

Can I modify or cancel my reservation?

Yes. You may modify or cancel your reservation up to 24 hours before your dining time at no charge. Cancellations within 24 hours may be subject to a $50 per person fee. Groups of 7 or more require 48 hours' notice. Please call us at +1 (212) 555-0174 or email reservations@eclatdining.com.

Do you accommodate walk-in guests?

Walk-in seating is available at our bar on a limited basis. We cannot guarantee a table without a reservation, especially on Friday and Saturday evenings. We strongly encourage making a reservation in advance to ensure the best possible experience.

How long is a table typically held?

We hold reserved tables for 15 minutes past the reservation time. If you anticipate running late, please notify us as soon as possible — we'll do our best to accommodate you. If we haven't heard from you after 15 minutes, we may need to release your table.

Dining Experience

What is the dress code at ÉCLAT?

We request smart casual attire. Collared shirts for men are appreciated at dinner. We ask that guests refrain from athletic wear, sportswear, or beachwear. Ties and jackets are welcome but not required. Our priority is that you feel comfortable and elegant.

How long should I allow for my dining experience?

A typical three-course dinner lasts approximately 2 hours. The à la carte menu usually takes 1.5–2 hours depending on your selections. The 10-course tasting menu is a 3–3.5 hour experience. We believe dining should never feel rushed — take all the time you desire.

Is there a corkage fee for bringing my own wine?

Yes, the corkage fee is $65 per bottle, limited to two bottles per table. We have an extensive wine list curated by our sommelier Isabelle Blanc — we encourage you to explore our selection, which includes exceptional bottles at every price point.

Can you arrange special decorations for a celebration?

Absolutely! We love celebrating milestones with our guests. Please let us know about birthdays, anniversaries, proposals, or other special occasions when booking. We offer custom cake arrangements, floral centerpieces, and special table setups. Additional charges may apply. Contact us at least 72 hours in advance.

Menu & Dietary

Do you offer vegetarian or vegan options?

Yes. We have several vegetarian dishes on our regular menu and can prepare a fully vegan multi-course experience upon advance request. Please notify us of your dietary preference when making your reservation so our kitchen team can prepare an exceptional personalized menu for you.

How do you handle food allergies?

We take all food allergies very seriously. Please disclose all allergies and intolerances when booking. We accommodate common allergies including gluten, dairy, shellfish, nuts, and eggs. However, our kitchen is not allergen-free, so we cannot guarantee the complete absence of allergens. Please consult with your server upon arrival.

Can I see the menu before my reservation?

Our full menu is available on our website. Please note that our menu changes seasonally to feature the finest available ingredients. Some dishes may be substituted based on availability. The tasting menu evolves weekly based on Chef Laurent's latest creative explorations.

Events & Private Dining

What is the minimum group size for private dining?

Our private dining suite, Le Salon Privé, accommodates 8–18 guests. For smaller private groups (4–7), we can arrange semi-private sections in our main dining room. Cocktail receptions can accommodate up to 40 guests. For all private dining inquiries, please contact our events team.

Do you provide catering for off-site events?

Yes, we offer exclusive catering services for select corporate and private events in New York City. Our catering team brings the full ÉCLAT experience to your venue. Please contact our events team with a minimum of 4 weeks' notice to discuss your requirements.

How do I book a masterclass or special event?

All masterclasses and special dining events can be booked through our Events page or by contacting us directly. Payment is required at the time of booking to secure your spot. All special events have strict cancellation policies — please review them before booking.

Policies

Are children welcome at ÉCLAT?

Children aged 12 and above are welcome at all services. Due to the nature of our fine dining experience, we ask that young guests be comfortable with formal dining settings. Lunch service on weekdays tends to have a more relaxed atmosphere suitable for older children. Children's menus are available on request.

Do you offer a loyalty or rewards program?

Yes! Our ÉCLAT Rewards program offers points on every visit, exclusive member-only events, early access to reservations, and complimentary chef's tastings. Create an account on our website and start earning from your very first visit.

Is photography permitted in the restaurant?

Personal photography is welcome throughout the restaurant. We only ask that flash photography be avoided during dinner service out of respect for other guests, and that professional photography or commercial filming require prior written permission from our management team.

Still Have Questions?

Our team is available daily to assist with any inquiries. Don't hesitate to reach out — we're here to ensure your visit is extraordinary.
