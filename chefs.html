Executive Chef

Laurent Dubois

Chef Laurent Dubois is the creative genius behind ÉCLAT's distinctive cuisine. Born in Lyon, France — the gastronomic capital of the world — Laurent trained under three legendary Michelin-starred chefs before spending five formative years in Tokyo's finest kaiseki restaurants.

His unique philosophy of "French Soul, Asian Spirit" forms the foundation of ÉCLAT's identity. Every dish that leaves his kitchen is a testament to his belief that food is the most powerful form of human expression.
