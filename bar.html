An Intimate Sanctuary

Where Every Sip Tells a Story

Step into ÉCLAT's intimate bar — a sanctuary of rare spirits, house-crafted cocktails, and a cellar-deep wine list curated by our in-house sommelier. Low candlelight, velvet seating, and the soft clink of crystal set the tone for an evening that lingers long after the last sip.

Whether you arrive before dinner or stay well after, the bar is where ÉCLAT's magic begins. Each cocktail is conceived as a dish — built with intention, finished with precision, and served with the quiet confidence of a kitchen at the top of its craft.
