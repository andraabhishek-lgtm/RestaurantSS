A5 Wagyu Tenderloin

5.0 (248 reviews)
· 45 min prep
$89 per person

An extraordinary celebration of Japanese A5 Wagyu — the world's most prized beef. Our 8oz center-cut tenderloin is seared to perfection using the classic French rôtissage technique, developing a mahogany crust while maintaining a luxurious medium-rare center with a melt-in-your-mouth quality unlike anything else.

Accompanied by a rich black truffle reduction, micro herbs from our rooftop garden, roasted bone marrow, and seasonal accompaniments — this is the dish that defines ÉCLAT.

Key Ingredients

A5 Wagyu Black Truffle Bone Marrow Micro Herbs Truffle Jus Shallots Red Wine Reduction

Allergens

Calories

~680 kcal

Sommelier Recommends

Château Pétrus 2016 — Pomerol AOC · $220/glass

Reserve & Order

Preparation Method

Seared over a 700°F Josper charcoal grill for 2 minutes each side, then rested for 10 minutes for optimal temperature distribution.

Source & Origin

Certified A5 Wagyu from the Kagoshima Prefecture of Japan. BMS score 10+ — the absolute pinnacle of marbling quality.

Chef's Note

"This dish is my love letter to the extraordinary. Every time I plate it, I feel the same reverence for the ingredient that I felt the first time I tasted A5 Wagyu in Kagoshima."

— Chef Laurent Dubois
